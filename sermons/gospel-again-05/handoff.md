## Notes

- AI disclosure: not needed. The footage and voice are the real sermon; only
  subtitles, a title bar and crops are added.
- carbon-monoxide names the actor 조인성 (an article joking that he has
  "연예인병"). It is a light aside, but it is a real person's name in a Short.
- late-coworker: the second half is a story he says he made up ("가상의
  스토리를 하나 만들자면"). That line is kept, so the real boy is not shown as
  the man who hates his coworker. Both long clips (late-coworker, good-anger)
  run close to 2 min.
- Transcription fixes in the subtitles: 블랙버스트 → 블록버스터, 넷플렉스 → 넷플릭스,
  잃지 → 읽지, 정지 → 정죄, 수능 → 순응, 싶다면/된다면 → 싶다며/된다며,
  데빗 → 데이비드 폴리슨, 때 → 떼, 겹겹히 → 겹겹이, 극률 → 긍휼.
- Other strong moments in this sermon, not cut yet: 9:28 two roads to
  happiness, the eldest child scolding the younger one, "야 엄마 얼마나
  고생하는지 아냐?" (~60 s, 9:28-10:30); 28:53 "겸손은 교만과 함께 갈 수 있지만
  교만은 교만과 함께 갈 수 없습니다" (~40 s); 32:25 the prayer about the 여명학교
  (a school for North Korean defectors) facing local opposition, "저것들 자기들
  땅값 올리려고...라는 판단은 바리새인을 만들어갑니다" (~55 s, touches a local
  dispute); 45:06 his army story: a struggling private hoping for a medical
  discharge, the sergeant who throws his bag at him (with a swear word), "너는
  너를 속이고 있다", then first place in the unit and "고상섭이 너는 정말 군생활을
  잘했다" (~6 min, too long for one Short; best as a two-part clip or a longer edit).
