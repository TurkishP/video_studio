## Notes

- AI disclosure: not needed. The footage and voice are the real sermon; only
  subtitles, a title bar and crops are added.
- lotto-first-prize runs 93 s: a full story that earns the extra time. Its
  first cut drops the aside about Netflix sermon illustrations and where he
  heard the story.
- risk-your-life jumps from 55:15 to 56:20 over the Galatians reading.
- Transcription fixes in the subtitles: 페스트/전염병 (whisper: 패스트/전년병),
  disordered love (Disorderly the love), 타인 지향적 (타인지향석).
- Other strong moments in this sermon, not cut yet:
  - 25:39 Barry Bonds and the Hall of Fame: steroids to protect his name,
    ending on Rebecca Pippert, "무엇이든지 우리를 지배하는 것이 우리의
    주인입니다" (~85 s). Whisper wrote 약물 as 양무 and 피펏 as 버켓.
  - 30:12 "저는 우상숭배가 없습니다 하는 사람은 이미 우상숭배를 하고 있다";
    idols work like addiction, "언제든지 끊을 수 있어" (~45 s).
  - 41:09 개 팔자가 상팔자, but nobody wants to be called a dog: food and
    comfort don't make us human (~60 s; cut the stroller slide at 41:43).
  - 10:15 coming back to church after years away and crying at the first
    song, "하나님이 나를 기다리고 계셨구나": repentance with joy, not fear (~45 s).
  - 31:03 Wall Street, "Greed is good" (~40 s).
  - 33:21 Humble Calvinism, his own blurb: "비록 제가 썼지만 너무 멋진
    문장이죠" (funny, but needs the TULIP context).
  - 38:12 Political ideology as an idol. Strong, but it includes his view of
    the martial-law declaration, so I left it out.
- god-was-waiting was added later, on the PC: repentance with joy, not fear.
